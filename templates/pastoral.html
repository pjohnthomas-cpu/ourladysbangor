Pastoral Plan

Please read Into the Future IV and share your feedback

The Diocese of Wrexham has published the 30-page Pastoral Plan document, Into the Future IV. Parishioners are warmly invited to download the document, read it carefully, and send their thoughts and feedback to the Parish Office by Thursday the 17th of September(link/email id below).

Download Pastoral Plan PDF Send Feedback

To help formulate our response, a number of short meetings will be held at the following times. Everyone is warmly invited to attend and contribute their thoughts on the 4 key questions the Bishop poses for parishes:

  • What is the mission of our parish?
  • How is the ongoing formation of adults undertaken in our parish or deanery, and what would help individuals in their ongoing formation?
  • Do we have in place a Finance Committee and Pastoral Council which promotes the co-responsibility of priest and people for the mission of the Church?
  • How do we promote stewardship of time, talent and treasure amongst the faithful?

Our Parish response needs to be submitted to the Bishop on Friday 18 th September, so meetings will be held as follows:

  1. Monday 14 th September: 2pm in person at Our Lady and St James; 7.30pm in person following 7pm Mass
  2. Tuesday 15 th September: 10.30 am in person following 10am Mass; 1pm in person at Ysgol Santes Helen, Caernarfon
  3. Thursday 17 th September: 8pm via Zoom. (Please email jo.charles2@btinternet.com for the link.)

If you are not able to attend but wish to contribute, please send your comments on the questions to the Parish office via priestbangor@rcdwxm.org.uk by Thursday 17 th September.
