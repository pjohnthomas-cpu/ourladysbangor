Notices & Announcements

Parish life — November 2026

Remembering with Gratitude, Waiting with Hope

November begins with the Solemnity of All Saints on 1 November and All Souls’ Day on 2 November. We rejoice in the saints and lovingly remember our departed family members, friends and parishioners. Offering Holy Mass for the faithful departed is one of the greatest expressions of that love. During this month of remembrance, Fr. Savi warmly encourages you to request a Mass for someone you love, whether for the departed, the sick, families or special intentions. Envelopes are available in the church and may be placed in the box provided.

Pope’s Intention for November

Let us pray for the proper use of wealth, that, not succumbing to the temptation of selfishness, it may always be put at the service of the common good and solidarity of those who have less.

First Holy Communion in Caernarfon — 15 November

First Holy Communion will be celebrated in Caernarfon on Sunday 15 November at 12:30. Please keep our First Communicants and their families in your prayers. Weekend Masses: Saturday 14 November, 17:00 Vigil Mass in Bangor (no Vigil Mass in Caernarfon); Sunday 15 November, 12:30 First Holy Communion Mass in Caernarfon; no Mass in Bangor at 17:00. Please share this with anyone who may be affected.

Priests’ Retreat — Tuesday 24 November

There will be no 10:00 Holy Mass in Bangor on Tuesday 24 November, as the priests of the Vicariate will be on retreat at the Loreto Spirituality Centre in Llandudno. Everyone is welcome to join the volunteer-led Rosary at 9:45. Please keep our priests in your prayers.

Thanksgiving Mass for Married Couples — 22 November

On Sunday 22 November at 10:00 we will celebrate a Thanksgiving Mass for couples marking a special anniversary in 2026. Couples celebrating a five-year milestone, from 5 to 60 years and beyond, are warmly invited. Please place your name and details in the box in the church so that we can remember you in our prayers. Family and friends are welcome.

Family Eucharist — 29 November

Our Family Eucharist will be celebrated on Sunday 29 November at 10:00, the First Sunday of Advent. A special blessing will be given to all those celebrating birthdays. Please complete a slip, available at the back of the church, and place it in the letterbox.

Sponsor a Christmas Tree for 2026

As Advent and Christmas approach, our church needs decorating for the season. If you would like to sponsor a Christmas tree, or share part of its cost, we would be most grateful. A tree can also be given in memory of a loved one or in thanksgiving for a special intention. Please speak to Fr. Savi or the parish office.

Advent and Christmas Decoration

Advent begins on Sunday 29 November. As last year, we invite volunteers to help plan and decorate the church for Advent and Christmas. If you would like to help, please get in touch with Chelsey David or Vanitha.

Advent Confessions in Porthmadog

On Friday 11 December, confessions will be available at the Church of the Most Holy Redeemer (Borth Road, Porthmadog, LL49 9BB) with several priests present from 11:00, followed by Holy Mass at 12 noon. All are encouraged to make use of this opportunity during Advent.

Diocesan Youth Day — 21 November

All youth are welcome at St Joseph’s Church, Colwyn Bay, on Saturday 21 November from 10:30, with Mass celebrated by Bishop Peter at 13:30. Under-18s require a parental consent form.

Making Jesus the Centre of Your Life at Uni

The Bangor Catholic Chaplaincy offers students and staff a welcoming community of friendship, support and hospitality. Weekly activities run on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays, with Sunday Movie Nights. Special events include guest speakers, a Vocations Evening, a church bake sale, a sponsored walk for Red Wednesday and local pilgrimages. New students are welcome to meet the team after Sunday Mass or to contact Ray, the University Chaplain.

Pastoral Visits to the Sick & Housebound

Bringing Holy Communion to those unable to attend Mass remains a parish priority. Please pass details to Fr. Savi or a parish team member if you or someone you know would value a visit.

Confirmation, First Holy Communion & RCIA

Confirmation preparation, First Holy Communion classes, and RCIA registration are all open. Forms are available at the back of church and on the parish website.

Sound System Upgrade in Bangor

The project to upgrade the parish sound system was sponsored in April by a generous donation of £5,000, and a further £2,000 was raised by the parish for additional equipment. We are now working with a new supplier. Thank you for your continued support and patience.

Donation & Giving Made Easier

By card or phone: use the card reader at the entrance of the church, or scan the QR code with your phone. UK taxpayers can complete Gift Aid once by email after paying by card, and it will apply to future donations on that card. By cheque: payable to Bangor and Caernarfon Catholic Church.

With Gratitude: New Chasubles and a New Cross

Four generous families sponsored new chasubles, which have now arrived, been blessed and are in active use. A new cross for Good Friday veneration, imported from Italy and sponsored by a generous family, was blessed on 4 October and now stands at the front in the aisle. Thank you to everyone who contributed; you are remembered in our prayers.

The Choir

The church has a voluntary choir which sings at the 10:00 Sunday Mass each week, as well as enriching the liturgy on days of obligation and major celebrations throughout the liturgical year.

The choir sings hymns, congregational mass settings, and occasional anthems. All are welcome to join.

To find out more, please speak with one of our choir members after the Sunday morning Mass, or contact our organist:
